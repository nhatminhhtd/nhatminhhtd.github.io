Privacy Policy — LiteNVR

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

LiteNVR is a personal, non-commercial network video recorder that runs locally on my own computer at home. This policy describes what data it uses when Google Drive backup is enabled.

Data we use

  • Camera recordings: video recorded from my own cameras on my home network. Recordings are stored on my local device, and — only if I enable Google Drive backup — a copy is uploaded to my own Google Drive account.
  • Google Drive access: LiteNVR uses the Google Drive API with the drive.file scope. This means it can only access files it created itself in my Drive. It cannot see, open, or modify my other Drive files.
  • Google account credentials: when I connect Google Drive, Google issues OAuth tokens to LiteNVR. Those tokens are stored only on my local NVR device and are used only to access my own Google Drive account. They are never shared with anyone else.

Data we do not collect

LiteNVR does not collect, store, share, or sell personal data about anyone else. It has no other users, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. No camera video or account information is sent to any third party except my own Google Drive account, as described above.

How to disconnect and delete data

  • To stop Drive backup, open LiteNVR, go to the Google Drive backup settings, and click Disconnect.
  • To remove LiteNVR's access to Google Drive entirely, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove access for LiteNVR.
  • To delete recordings already uploaded to Google Drive, delete the LiteNVR folder (or individual files) in Google Drive.
  • To delete recordings stored locally, delete them from the LiteNVR interface or from local storage on the NVR device.

Contact

LiteNVR is a personal project for my own home use. For questions about this policy, contact me through the support email listed in the Google Cloud project for this app.
