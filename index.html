LiteNVR — Personal Home NVR

LiteNVR is a personal, non-commercial network video recorder (NVR) that runs locally on a computer in my own home. It records video from my own IP cameras on my home network, and lets me view live video and recordings from home.

What it does

  • Records RTSP video streams from my own cameras to local storage.
  • Shows live video and playback in a local web interface.
  • Optionally backs up recordings I choose to my own Google Drive, using the Google Drive API with the drive.file scope. LiteNVR can only see and manage files it created itself in my Drive; it cannot see my other Drive files.

What it does not do

  • It is not a public service and it has no other users.
  • It does not collect, share, or sell any personal data.
  • It does not send camera video to any third party except my own Google Drive account, only when I enable Drive backup.
  • Google account credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored only on my local NVR device. They are never shared with anyone.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for details on what data is used, how to disconnect Google Drive, and how to request deletion of data.
